# Head Room changelog

## 0.2.1

- Save multiple measured heads and imported head STLs on the same device. Each save adds a separate profile, even when two heads share a name.
- Choose a profile under **Your saved heads** to preview it and start helmet try-on.
- Your previously saved head from 0.1.0 or 0.2.0 appears in the picker automatically.

## 0.2.0

- Open a new or saved head and try on a helmet STL. Align the shell and inspect the fit in 3D or in front and side sections.
- Scale the helmet uniformly or by axis, estimate room for padding, and open the fitted helmet in OrcaSlicer.
- The fitting view fills the tab. The welcome screen includes suggested measuring tools and Uncle Jessy’s printable cosplay calipers.

## 0.1.0

- Create a reference head from four guided measurements. The highlighted guide and head shape update as you work.
- Import a head STL, save a head on the device, and export it as an STL.
